# AIYouMe Logo Accessibility Notes

## Approved Master

- Final symbol: `/public/brand/aiyoume-symbol.svg`
- Final lockup: `/public/brand/aiyoume-lockup.svg`
- Site-integrated mark: `/public/brand/aiyoume-mark.svg`
- Site-integrated logotype: `/public/brand/aiyoume-logo-dark.svg`

## Current Rules

- The mark is a literal `Y`.
- `You` is always the left leaf.
- `AI` is always the stem and seed at the center.
- `Me` is always the right leaf.
- Meaning is carried by position and silhouette, using a clearer sprout-like `Y`.

## Why This Is Safer

- Meaning is encoded by position first.
- Meaning is also encoded by the left leaf, center stem-and-seed, and right leaf geometry.
- Shape differences remain visible in grayscale and in common color-vision deficiencies.
- The mono variants can be used where color contrast is constrained.

## Runway Exploration Guidance

If this moves into image generation, treat the vector assets above as the master source. Runway should be used to explore:

- motion identity around the `Y` intersection
- material and lighting directions
- brand key visuals for hero sections or ads

Do not replace the master logo with a raster-generated image.
